FAQ

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. No card needed, no time limit. You only hit a limit at 3 clients, 5 projects or 10 invoices.

What happens when I upgrade?

Your limits are removed instantly and all your existing data stays exactly where it is.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from Settings → Plan → Manage billing. You keep your data and drop back to the Free plan limits.

How do my clients pay me?

On Pro and Business, every invoice gets a secure Stripe pay link. Clients pay by card and the invoice is marked paid automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each account can only see its own data, enforced at the database level. You can export everything to CSV.
